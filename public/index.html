HOOKtopus

Get a URL. Point a webhook at it. Watch every request arrive, live and byte for byte, then send it wherever you need it.

  • Bodies are kept as the exact bytes sent, even when they are broken.
  • Headers keep their order, their case and their duplicates.
  • Forward any request to a public URL, or to localhost with the CLI.
  • Bins expire after 24 hours. No account, no tracking.

Forward to your machine

npx hooktopus forward http://localhost:3000

Creates a bin, prints its URL, and replays everything it receives against your local server.
